# Archived guides

These 22 guides were removed from the product on 2026-08-24. They are kept here, out of
the nav and out of every build, so a decision can be reversed by moving a file back and
adding one nav line — not by digging through git history.

## Why

The project is for **someone stranded in the wilderness who needs to survive**. Measured
against that, the corpus had drifted into being an encyclopedia about the topic of
survival. The clearest symptom: the guides stated their own priority — 3 minutes without
air, 3 hours without shelter, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food — and then gave
food more than twice the coverage of water.

## What went, and why

**Not wilderness at all.** `urban-survival`, `natural-disaster`, `home-preparedness`,
`communication-plans`, `financial-preparedness`. Building collapse, hurricanes, household
water drums, insurance paperwork. A different project.

**Long-term living.** `food-preservation`, `field-butchering`, `long-term-structures`,
`clothing-and-insulation`, `improvised-tools`, `containers-and-vessels`. Cold smoking,
root cellaring, log cabins, brain tanning, stone knapping, fired clay pots. All measured
in weeks or months. None of it inside a survival window.

**Food you will not get.** `hunting-basics` — its own table put improvised-weapon success
under 5%. `cooking-without-gear` — you are boiling water, not cooking meals.

**Plant and fungus identification.** `edible-plants-temperate`,
`edible-plants-tropical`, `mushroom-identification`. Species identification from written
description is unreliable in the direction that kills: hemlock passes for wild carrot,
death camas for wild onion. You have three weeks without food. The expected value is
negative. `foraging-basics` now says so plainly instead.

**Primitive and overlapping fire.** `friction-methods` (its own figure: 15-60 hours to
competence), `fire-from-nothing` (overlapped the other two), `maintaining-fire` (banking
coals and carrying fire between camps). `spark-methods` and `fire-in-wet-conditions`
survive and carry the methods that actually work when stranded.

**Marginal or redundant.** `group-dynamics` (expedition management), `solo-survival`
(overlapped `survival-mindset`), `water-storage` (caching and 55-gallon drums).

## Content rescued before archiving

Three pieces were verified against the literature and moved rather than lost:

- **Rabbit starvation / lean meat** — from `hunting-basics` into `foraging-basics`. A
  diet of only lean protein kills faster than fasting, and small game is nearly all lean
  protein. It applies to snared game and lean fish equally, so the food hub is the right
  home.
- **Tularemia from handling rabbits** — from `field-butchering` into
  `trapping-and-snares`, which is where someone is actually holding a carcass.
- **Primitive fire's real cost** — the 15-60 hour figure now appears in `spark-methods`
  and the glossary, so the technique is described honestly rather than simply vanishing.

## Your phone can reach a satellite with no signal

**Dial emergency services even with zero bars.** iPhone 14 and later, Pixel 9 and 10, and
recent Galaxy phones switch to satellite on their own when no cell tower answers. No app,
no setup, nothing to remember. Free for the life of the phone on iPhone.

!!! warning "It will not work under trees"
    It needs open sky. A canopy, canyon, or cave blocks it — which is where people get
    lost. **A failed attempt under cover does not mean your phone cannot do it.**

    Move to open ground and try again: a clearing, ridgeline, gravel bar, or lake edge.
    Mark your route. Hold still, follow the on-screen arrow, and give it a few minutes.

    Send early, while the battery is good.

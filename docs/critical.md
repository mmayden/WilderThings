# Critical

> Facts that change what you do — and that you would never think to search for.

!!! danger "The rule for this page"
    Everything here is something where **what most people would do makes it worse.**

---

## Getting found

**Your phone can reach a satellite with no signal.**
Dial emergency services even with zero bars. iPhone 14+, Pixel 9/10, and recent Galaxy
phones switch to satellite on their own when no tower answers. No app, no setup.
[Detail](navigation/gps-and-electronics.md#satellite-sos-built-into-your-phone)

??? warning "It will not work under trees — which is where people get lost"
    The link needs open sky. A canopy, canyon, or cave blocks it. **A failed attempt under
    cover does not mean your phone cannot do it.** Move to a clearing, ridgeline, gravel
    bar, or lake edge, marking your route. Hold still, follow the on-screen arrow, give it
    a few minutes. Send early, while the battery is good.

**A compass stored near your phone can point south.**
Magnets in cases, mounts, and speakers re-magnetise a needle backwards. It swings freely,
settles confidently, and is wrong by 180°. Check it against the sun or Polaris.
[Detail](navigation/map-and-compass.md#check-the-compass-itself-first)

**You cannot walk straight without something to aim at.**
With no visible reference, people walk in circles as tight as 65 ft across while certain
they are going straight. Sight on a distant object, walk to it, pick the next.
[Detail](navigation/natural-navigation.md#why-you-need-any-of-this)

## Medical

**Being late is not a reason to skip rabies treatment.**
There is no deadline. It is given whenever you arrive, provided symptoms have not started.
Four days out is still worth the trip. [Detail](wildlife/wolves-and-canids.md#rabies-risk)

**Bat contact counts, even with no visible bite.**
Bat bites are often too small to see or feel. Waking with a bat in the room is treated as
an exposure. [Detail](wildlife/wolves-and-canids.md#rabies-risk)

**Antihistamines do not stop anaphylaxis.**
Only epinephrine does. Benadryl manages mild reactions and will not save someone whose
airway is closing. [Detail](wildlife/insect-threats.md)

**A bandana is not a tourniquet.**
A cloth band alone cannot reach arterial pressure. Tied tight it stops the veins and not
the artery, so the bleeding gets **worse**. A tourniquet needs a rigid windlass.
[Detail](medical/first-aid-basics.md#step-3-tourniquet)

**Do not strap anyone to an improvised backboard.**
Rigid immobilisation is no longer recommended and can worsen outcomes. Keep them still,
warm, and padded instead. [Detail](medical/fractures-and-splints.md#what-to-do-instead)

## Water and food

**Bleach does not kill cryptosporidium.**
Neither does iodine. If crypto is a concern — and it is in any water animals reach — boil
or filter. [Detail](water/purification.md#household-bleach-sodium-hypochlorite)

**Cooking does not make every fish safe.**
Ciguatera survives cooking, freezing, drying and smoking. No taste, no smell, no field
test, no antidote. Avoid barracuda, moray, and large reef predators.
[Detail](food/fishing-improvised.md#fish-that-poison-you)

**A diet of only lean meat kills faster than eating nothing.**
Rabbit and most small game are nearly pure protein, and the liver cannot clear the
nitrogen fast enough. Save the fat and marrow.
[Detail](food/foraging-basics.md#lean-meat-alone-will-not-sustain-you)

**Never eat a snail or slug raw.**
Rat lungworm migrates to the brain and has killed healthy adults from a single raw slug.
Purge, then boil ten minutes. [Detail](food/insect-foraging.md#snails-and-slugs)

## Shelter and weather

**A hammock is colder than the ground, not warmer.**
Your weight crushes the insulation beneath you flat. People get dangerously cold at 70°F
(21°C). You need an underquilt or a pad, or the ground.
[Detail](shelter/tarp-and-poncho.md)

**Ground insulation matters more than the roof.**
Cold ground pulls heat out of you all night. Without it, walls and a roof are close to
worthless. [Detail](shelter/shelter-principles.md#ground-insulation-is-priority-one)

**There is no safe position to adopt outdoors in a thunderstorm.**
The lightning crouch was withdrawn — not because the posture harms you, but because
believing you have a protective position delays you seeking real shelter.
[Detail](climate-specific/mountain-survival.md#lightning-safety)

## Vehicles

**In a sinking car, get out in the first seconds.**
The advice to wait for the pressure to equalise will drown you. **Seatbelts. Window. Out.
Children first.** Call for help from outside the car.
[Detail](preparedness/vehicle-kit.md#escaping-a-sinking-vehicle)

**Your window breaker may not work on your car.**
Nothing tested breaks laminated glass, and about a third of recent models have it. Check
the label in the corner of each side window — now, not while it fills with water.
[Detail](preparedness/vehicle-kit.md)

# Recommended Survival Reading List

> Curated reading list organized by category. These books form the foundation of wilderness survival, emergency medicine, and preparedness knowledge.

## Core Survival Manuals

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **SAS Survival Handbook** | John "Lofty" Wiseman | The single most comprehensive general survival reference, covering every environment and scenario. |
| **FM 21-76 (FM 3-05.70) Army Survival Manual** | US Department of the Army | Systematic military survival doctrine covering evasion, shelter, water, food, and navigation in all climates. |
| **Hawke's Green Beret Survival Manual** | Mykel Hawke | Practical survival strategies from a Special Forces veteran, with clear decision-making frameworks. |
| **98.6 Degrees: The Art of Keeping Your Ass Alive** | Cody Lundin | Thermoregulation-focused survival philosophy emphasizing core temperature as the primary survival priority. |
| **Build the Perfect Bug Out Bag** | Creek Stewart | Step-by-step emergency kit building with gear lists and load-out strategies for 72-hour preparedness. |

## Bushcraft and Skills

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Bushcraft 101** | Dave Canterbury | Foundational bushcraft skills organized around the "5 Cs of Survivability" (cutting, combustion, cover, container, cordage). |
| **Advanced Bushcraft** | Dave Canterbury | Deeper techniques for long-term wilderness living, including trapping, hide tanning, and tool construction. |
| **Tom Brown's Field Guide to Wilderness Survival** | Tom Brown Jr. | Nature awareness and primitive skills from a legendary tracker, with strong emphasis on the spiritual dimension of survival. |
| **Camping and Woodcraft** | Horace Kephart | Classic early-20th-century reference on camp craft, cooking, and backcountry travel technique. |
| **Primitive Technology: A Survivalist's Guide to Building Tools, Shelters, and More in the Wild** | John Plant | Visual guide to building structures, tools, and implements from raw natural materials with no modern tools. |

## Wilderness Medicine

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Wilderness Medicine** (Textbook) | Paul Auerbach | The definitive medical reference for austere environments, covering every injury and illness encountered far from hospitals. |
| **Medicine for the Outdoors** | Paul Auerbach | A condensed, field-practical version of the full textbook, written for non-physicians. |
| **NOLS Wilderness Medicine** | Tod Schimelpfenig | Scenario-based wilderness medical training used in NOLS and WMA certification courses. |
| **Where There Is No Doctor** | David Werner | Community health manual for situations without access to physicians, covering diagnosis, treatment, and prevention. |
| **Wilderness & Travel Medicine: A Comprehensive Guide** | Eric Weiss | Pocket-sized field reference for backcountry medical emergencies. |

## Psychology and Decision-Making

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Deep Survival: Who Lives, Who Dies, and Why** | Laurence Gonzales | Understanding the psychology of survival, why some people survive and others do not, and how the brain functions under extreme stress. |
| **The Unthinkable: Who Survives When Disaster Strikes and Why** | Amanda Ripley | How ordinary people respond to disasters, the stages of the survival arc (denial, deliberation, action), and training the brain for crisis. |
| **Surviving the Extremes** | Kenneth Kamler | Medical and psychological case studies from the most extreme environments on Earth. |
| **The Survivor's Club** | Ben Sherwood | Research-based exploration of the traits, behaviors, and luck that determine survival outcomes. |

## Navigation and Weather

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Wilderness Navigation** | Bob Burns, Mike Burns | Map and compass fundamentals, GPS use, and route-finding for backcountry travel. |
| **Be Expert with Map and Compass** | Bjorn Kjellstrom | The classic orienteering manual, thorough on declination, bearings, and triangulation. |
| **Weather Forecasting (BSA Merit Badge Series)** | Boy Scouts of America | Accessible introduction to reading clouds, barometric pressure, and predicting weather changes in the field. |

## Foraging and Wild Food

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Edible Wild Plants: A North American Field Guide** | Thomas Elias, Peter Dykeman | Photographic identification of 200+ edible species with habitat, season, and preparation details. |
| **Peterson Field Guide to Medicinal Plants and Herbs** | Steven Foster, James Duke | Identification and traditional uses of medicinal plants in eastern and central North America. |
| **The Forager's Harvest** | Samuel Thayer | In-depth profiles of wild edible plants with practical harvesting and preparation methods based on firsthand experience. |
| **Stalking the Wild Asparagus** | Euell Gibbons | The classic foraging narrative that launched modern wild food interest, with recipes and plant profiles. |
| **Mushrooms Demystified** | David Arora | The comprehensive mushroom identification reference for North America. |

## Preparedness and Self-Reliance

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **The Prepper's Blueprint** | Tess Pennington | Structured preparedness planning from short-term emergencies through long-term societal disruption. |
| **When All Hell Breaks Loose** | Cody Lundin | Urban and suburban disaster preparedness with emphasis on practical, low-cost solutions. |
| **Emergency: This Book Will Save Your Life** | Neil Strauss | One journalist's journey through survival training, providing an accessible entry point to the preparedness world. |

## Fire and Primitive Skills

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Fire Making: The Forgotten Art of Conjuring Flame with Spark, Tinder, and Skill** | Daniel Hume | Dedicated deep-dive into fire making, from primitive friction methods to modern ignition tools, by a practitioner who learned the techniques from the cultures that still use them. |
| **Participating in Nature** | Thomas Elpel | Primitive living skills including brain-tanning, bow making, and friction fire, taught through immersive outdoor experience. |

## Water and Shelter

| Title | Author | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **How to Stay Alive in the Woods** | Bradford Angier | Classic guide to wilderness self-sufficiency covering shelter, water, fire, and food with time-tested techniques. |
| **The Complete Walker IV** | Colin Fletcher, Chip Rawlins | Exhaustive backpacking reference covering gear selection, campcraft, and long-distance hiking logistics. |
